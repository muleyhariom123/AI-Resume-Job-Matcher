💡 Understanding the difference: Textual similarity measures full vocabulary alignment across all terms in both documents, whereas Skill Coverage measures how many specific technical keywords from the job description were identified in your resume.

Mathematical Formula & Vector Space Model:
The application uses TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) Vectorization coupled with Cosine Similarity:
Score = cos(θ) × 100 = (v_resume • v_job) / (||v_resume|| × ||v_job||) × 100%
  • Term Frequency (TF): Measures how frequently a word appears in the document.
  • Inverse Document Frequency (IDF): Downweights ubiquitous words while magnifying informative technical keywords.
  • Cosine Similarity: Evaluates the cosine of the angle between two high-dimensional vectors in vocabulary space.
  • Why a score may be low: Because TF-IDF evaluates lexical vocabulary overlap, slight differences in phrasing (e.g. "implemented scalable storage" vs "designed fault-tolerant database architecture") lower vector alignment, even if the candidate possesses equivalent experience.
